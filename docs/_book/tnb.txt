                        Reader's Warning

  如果你正在服用降糖药物，未经医生许可，请勿擅自更改饮食规
律。本书中的治疗糖尿病饮食规划会降低血糖水平，因此，你可能需
要减少服药剂量，以免药物过量导致严重的低血糖反应。实施本项饮
食规划的前几周，请务必告知医生并密切监测血糖。
     If you are taking medication to lower your blood sugar, do not change
your eating habits without your doctor's permission. The diabetes treatment
diet plan in this book will lower your blood sugar levels; therefore, you may
need to reduce your medication dosage to avoid an overdose that could lead
to severe hypoglycemia. Be sure to inform your doctor and closely monitor
your blood sugar levels during the first few weeks of implementing this diet
plan.

  如果你同时还在服用降血压药物，本书中的饮食规划可能会大幅
降低你的血压，请务必监测血压，有任何病情变化立即告知医生。
   If you are also taking blood pressure medication, the dietary plan in this
book may significantly lower your blood pressure. Please be sure to
monitor your blood pressure and inform your doctor immediately of any
changes in your condition.

2050年，每3个成年人中就会有1人患上糖尿病。

    Today, 26 million Americans (11.3% of adults) have diabetes, and 80
million (35%) have prediabetes. If this trend continues, by 2050, one in
three adults will have diabetes.

  而这场危机的根源是——不健康的美国饮食。医学委员会乃至美
国糖尿病协会（ADA）都放弃了饮食治疗Ⅱ型糖尿病的方法，药物治

的体重增加，胰岛素耐受增加，最终导致糖尿病发病率增高。
    The root of this crisis is the unhealthy American diet. Medical boards
and even the American Diabetes Association (ADA) have abandoned
dietary therapy for type 2 diabetes; medication is now the accepted
treatment. However, even medication itself can cause significant weight
gain and increased insulin resistance, ultimately leading to a higher
incidence of diabetes.

  药物控制血糖水平的同时，却不断摄入标准的美国饮食，这样是
无法治愈糖尿病的。人体过度肥胖是导致Ⅱ型糖尿病的最主要致病因
素。如果你减去身体多余的脂肪，维持体内高水平的微量营养素，机
体细胞对胰岛素会更加敏感，这样就能治愈Ⅱ型糖尿病。
    Controlling blood sugar levels with medication while continuously
consuming a standard American diet will not cure diabetes. Excessive
obesity is the leading cause of type 2 diabetes. If you lose excess body fat
and maintain high levels of micronutrients, your body's cells will become
more sensitive to insulin, thus potentially curing type 2 diabetes.

  想要治疗Ⅱ型糖尿病，就必须遵循正确的饮食和锻炼规划，这不
仅能减肥，还能让身体从疾病中恢复过来。营养学家饮食规划富含微

第一步——什么是糖尿病
Step 1 – What is Diabetes?

糖尿病大多数是遗传的吗？全家都超重不是我的错。

      来说，遗传只是危险因素之一。尽管父母肥胖的人罹患肥胖症
      的风险增加了10倍，但近几十年来，肥胖症的猛增，还是由于
      快餐和加工食品导致的。从根本上来说，肥胖不是遗传的。
      While diabetes has a high heritability, genetics is only one of the
      risk factors for those with predisposed dietary habits. Although
      people whose parents are obese have a 10 times higher risk of
      developing obesity, the surge in obesity in recent decades is largely
      due to fast food and processed foods. Fundamentally, obesity is not
      hereditary.
      因此，是食物的选择、运动不足和基因共同决定了肥胖和糖尿
      病。最新研究表明，肥胖的传播来自于朋友圈中相似的饮食方
      式。导致糖尿病的主要因素不是遗传而是你的生活方式。在社
      交中，与那些能和你分享健康饮食方式的人交朋友。
      Therefore, food choices, lack of exercise, and genetics collectively
      determine obesity and diabetes. Recent research suggests that
      obesity spreads through similar eating habits within one's social
      circle. The primary cause of diabetes is not genetics but your
      lifestyle. In your social life, befriend those who share healthy eating
      habits with you.

酒精的作用如何——我应该喝点红酒来保护心脏吗？

      酒精对心脏健康没有益处。它有抗血栓形成的作用，与阿司匹
      林相似。除非你正在吃不健康饮食，否则想用酒精或葡萄酒来
      给血液“瘦身”对健康没有一点帮助。红酒含有有益化合物，如
      黄酮类和白藜芦醇，它们是葡萄皮中的一种有效抗氧化剂。葡
      萄、浆果和其他植物型食物中也含有这样的有益化合物。因
      此，你不需要通过喝葡萄酒来获益。
      Alcohol offers no benefits to heart health. While it has an anti-
      thrombotic effect, similar to aspirin, it doesn't help your blood by
      using alcohol or wine unless you're eating an unhealthy diet. Red
      wine contains beneficial compounds such as flavonoids and
      resveratrol, which are effective antioxidants found in grape skins.
      These beneficial compounds are also found in grapes, berries, and
      other plant-based foods. Therefore, you don't need to benefit from
      drinking wine.
      每天喝酒超过一杯将会增加腰部脂肪，诱发其他健康方面问
      题，如，乳腺癌。
      Drinking more than one glass of alcohol a day can increase
      abdominal fat and trigger other health problems, such as breast
      cancer.
吃有机食物对健康很重要吗？
Is eating organic food important for health?
      我们之所以关注非有机食品是因为农药问题。科学家们认为，
      农药在农产品中的低水平残留是微不足道的。12种含农药浓度
      最低的食物是洋葱、甜玉米、菠萝、鳄梨、卷心菜、甜豌豆
      （冷冻）、芦笋、芒果、茄子、猕猴桃、哈密瓜和红薯。12种
      含农药浓度最高的食物是苹果、芹菜、草莓、油桃、桃子、葡
      萄、菠菜、黄瓜、蓝莓、生菜和土豆。
      Our concern about non-organic foods stems from pesticide issues.
      Scientists believe that the low levels of pesticide residues in
      agricultural products are negligible. The 12 foods with the lowest
      pesticide concentrations are onions, sweet corn, pineapples,
      avocados, cabbage, frozen sweet peas, asparagus, mangoes,
      eggplants, kiwis, cantaloupes, and sweet potatoes. The 12 foods
      with the highest pesticide concentrations are apples, celery,
      strawberries, nectarines, peaches, grapes, spinach, cucumbers,
      blueberries, lettuce, and potatoes.
为什么不吃橄榄油？我还以为对我有好处。
Why not eat olive oil? I thought it would be good for me.
      油不是健康食物，它属于一种加工食品，高卡路里，低营养，
      而且不含纤维。富含单链不饱和脂肪酸的食物，如橄榄油，比
      富含饱和脂肪酸和反式脂肪酸的食物危害要小一些，但是危害
      小不代表没有危害。把坚果和种子作为脂肪摄入的来源，比油
      更好。
      Oil is not a healthy food; it's a processed food, high in calories, low
      in nutrients, and contains no fiber. Foods rich in monounsaturated
      fatty acids, such as olive oil, are less harmful than foods rich in
      saturated and trans fatty acids, but less harmful doesn't mean
      harmless. Nuts and seeds are better sources of fat than oil.
