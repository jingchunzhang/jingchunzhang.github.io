# Ebook Funnel Monetization

## Use this skill when
- You need to decide how a blog post or email sequence should make money.
- You want one content asset to support ads, affiliate revenue, and your independent site.

## Business model
1. Google ads
2. Independent site
3. Affiliate recommendation links

## Decision framework
### 1. Google ads
Use for:
- high-volume informational keywords
- broad education pages
- comparison or explainer content with strong dwell time potential

### 2. Independent site
Use for:
- readers showing solution intent
- product, toolkit, service, or premium bundle offers
- readers who have already consumed the free ebook or follow-up sequence

### 3. Affiliate links
Use for:
- adjacent products or tools that genuinely help the reader
- listicles, comparisons, recommended resources, or setup guides
- email follow-ups after trust is established

## Output requirements
For each ebook-derived blog idea, define:
- primary search intent
- primary monetization path
- secondary monetization path
- CTA locations
- conversion event to measure

## CTA placement rules
- Above the fold: problem-solution hook
- Mid-content: ebook opt-in CTA
- After trust section: affiliate or independent-site CTA
- Footer / end CTA: strongest next step based on reader intent

## Trust and compliance rules
- Recommendations must be relevant and transparent.
- Do not overload every paragraph with monetization blocks.
- For health content, prioritize reader safety and credibility over aggressive conversion.

## Suggested KPIs
- organic clicks per article
- email opt-in rate
- ebook delivery completion rate
- independent-site click-through rate
- affiliate click-through rate
- revenue per article / per subscriber
