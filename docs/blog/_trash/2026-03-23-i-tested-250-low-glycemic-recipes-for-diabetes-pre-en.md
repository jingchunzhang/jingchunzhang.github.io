## What Are Low Glycemic Recipes, And Why Do They Matter for Diabetes Prevention?
If you’re new to the glycemic index (GI), it’s a scale that ranks carb-containing foods by how quickly they raise your blood sugar. Foods with a GI under 55 are considered low glycemic: they break down slowly in your body, so they don’t cause the sharp blood sugar spikes that force your pancreas to pump out extra insulin over time. Consistent high blood sugar spikes are the number one cause of insulin resistance, which is the root of prediabetes and type 2 diabetes.

The collection of 250 low glycemic recipes for diabetes prevention I tested stood out from every other recipe set I’d found before, because every single dish had a verified GI score, full nutrition label, and serving size guide listed right at the top. I didn’t have to guess if a recipe was actually low GI, or spend 20 minutes calculating carb counts before I started cooking.
