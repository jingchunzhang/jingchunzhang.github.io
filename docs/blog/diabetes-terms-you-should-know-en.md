# Diabetes Terms You Should Know (Without the Confusion)

> **[中文版](./diabetes-terms-you-should-know)** | **[Chinese Version](./diabetes-terms-you-should-know)**

Many people feel lost not because they ignore care—but because medical language is hard.

## Five terms worth mastering first
- **HbA1c**: your average glucose trend over ~3 months.
- **Fasting glucose**: baseline blood sugar after overnight fasting.
- **Postprandial glucose**: blood sugar after eating.
- **Hypoglycemia**: low blood sugar episode requiring quick response.
- **Insulin resistance**: reduced response to insulin signaling.

## How to use a glossary effectively
1. Map each term to your own lab report.
2. Bring 3–5 unfamiliar terms to your next clinic visit.
3. Keep a personal “health translation note” in your phone.
