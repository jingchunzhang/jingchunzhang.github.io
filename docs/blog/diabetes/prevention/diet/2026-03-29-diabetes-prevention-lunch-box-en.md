## Why Lunch Boxes Matter for Diabetes Prevention

For office workers, lunch is often overlooked:

- **High-calorie takeout**: Regular takeout can be 800-1000 calories
- **Rapid blood sugar spikes**: Refined carbs cause quick glucose rises
- **Insufficient vegetables**: Takeout typically lacks veggies
- **Excessive oil and salt**: Flavorful dishes aren't friendly to blood sugar control

Bringing your own lunch solves all these problems!

## The Golden Ratio for Healthy Lunch Boxes

### The 2:1:1 Rule

A perfect diabetes-friendly lunch box:

- **1/2 Vegetables**: Non-starchy veggies
- **1/4 Protein**: Quality protein
- **1/4 Grains**: Low GI whole grains

### Detailed Breakdown

| Category | Portion | Calories |
|----------|---------|----------|
| Cooked grains | 100-150g | 130-200 |
| Protein | 80-100g | 100-150 |
| Vegetables | 150-200g | 50-80 |
| Oils | 1 tsp | 40-45 |
| **Total** | - | **320-475** |

## 10 Classic Lunch Box Recipes

### 1. Brown Rice Chicken Breast Bento

**Ingredients:**
- 100g brown rice
- 100g chicken breast
- 80g broccoli
- 50g carrots
- 1 tsp olive oil

**Instructions:**
1. Soak brown rice overnight, cook the next day
2. Season chicken with salt and pepper, grill or bake
3. Blanch broccoli and carrots
4. Arrange in bento box

**Calories: ~400**

## Bento Making Tips

### Weekend Bulk Prep

**Sunday 2-Hour Prep Schedule:**

| Time | Task | Content |
|------|------|---------|
| 9:00-10:00 | Cook grains | Brown rice, quinoa, divide into 5 portions |
| 10:00-11:00 | Prep proteins | Grill chicken, bake fish, boil eggs |
| 11:00-11:30 | Prep veggies | Wash, chop, bag |
| 11:30-12:00 | Assemble | Put into containers |

### Storage Tips

- **Refrigerate**: Store in fridge, good for 3-4 days
- **Reheat**: Microwave 2-3 minutes on high
- **Veggie trick**: Blanch briefly to keep color and texture

## Choosing the Right Bento Box

### Recommended Materials

1. **Glass Bento Box**
   - Pros: Safe, microwave-safe, easy to clean
   - Cons: Heavy, breakable
   - Brands: Pyrex, Corning

2. **Silicone Bento Box**
   - Pros: Lightweight, collapsible, unbreakable
   - Cons: Can't use over direct flame
   - Recommendation: Collapsible silicone boxes

3. **Stainless Steel Bento Box**
   - Pros: Durable, lightweight
   - Cons: Not microwave-safe
   - Recommendation: Insulated stainless boxes

### Size Selection

- Women: 600-800ml
- Men: 800-1000ml

## FAQ

### Q1: No microwave at work?

- Choose cold salad-style bento
- Use insulated lunch box
- Pour hot water over container in morning

### Q2: Vegetables turn yellow after heating?

- Blanch briefly, don't overcook
- Drain well before putting in bento
- Bring separate small sauce container

### Q3: Getting tired of the same food?

- Change main ingredient weekly
- Vary seasoning styles (Chinese, Japanese, Korean)
- Treat yourself to a restaurant meal occasionally
