## Why Freezer Meals for Diabetes Prevention

Freezer meal prep is the ultimate time management tool:

- **Save time**: 2 hours weekend = dinners for a month
- **Control portions**: Pre-portion to avoid overeating
- **Variety**: Can make multiple flavors at once
- **Stable blood sugar**: Carefully prepare low-GI meals

## 5 Freezer Dinner Recipes

### 1. Frozen Brown Rice Chicken Fried Rice

**Ingredients:**
- 150g frozen brown rice
- 100g frozen chicken breast
- 80g frozen veggies (carrots, corn, peas)
- 1 egg
- 1 tbsp soy sauce

**Instructions:**
1. Thaw rice in microwave 3 min
2. Dice chicken, microwave 2 min
3. Scramble egg in hot pan
4. Add rice and veggies, stir-fry
5. Add chicken, season and serve

### 2. Quinoa Salmon Bowl

**Ingredients:**
- 100g frozen quinoa
- 100g frozen salmon
- 50g blanched spinach
- Half a lemon

**Instructions:**
1. Heat quinoa in microwave 3 min
2. Bake salmon at 200°C for 10 min
3. Blanch spinach, arrange on plate
4. Squeeze lemon juice over

### 3. Tomato Beef Soup

**Ingredients:**
- 100g frozen beef
- 2 tomatoes
- Half onion
- 50g frozen carrots

**Instructions:**
1. Sauté tomatoes and onion
2. Add beef and carrots
3. Add water, simmer 20 min
4. Season and serve

### 4. Tofu Veggie Pan

**Ingredients:**
- 100g firm tofu
- 30g frozen shiitake mushrooms
- 100g bok choy
- Soy sauce, minced garlic

**Instructions:**
1. Cube tofu, pan-fry until golden
2. Add mushrooms, stir-fry
3. Add bok choy, cook until soft
4. Season and serve

### 5. Shrimp Broccoli

**Ingredients:**
- 80g frozen shrimp
- 100g frozen broccoli
- Little garlic
- Salt and pepper

**Instructions:**
1. Thaw shrimp and broccoli in microwave
2. Heat pan, sauté garlic
3. Add shrimp, cook until pink
4. Add broccoli, stir-fry
5. Season and serve

## Reheating Tips

### Microwave

- **Grains**: High 2-3 min, covered
- **Stir-fry**: High 2 min, add water
- **Soup**: High 3-4 min

### Oven

- **Meats**: 180°C, 10-15 min
- **Potatoes with skin**: 200°C, 20 min

### Steamer

- **Buns**: Steam 5 min after water boils
- **Fish**: Steam 8 min after water boils

## Freezer Prep Notes

### Container Selection

- Use airtight containers
- Glass or silicone recommended
- Portion individually for easy use

### Label Dates

- Always label with date
- First in, first out
- Don't eat after expiration

### Avoid

- Repeated freezing/thawing
- Leave at room temp over 2 hours
- Don't fill too full, leave expansion space

## FAQ

### Q1: Are frozen foods unhealthy?

**No**:
- Freezing is one of the healthiest preservation methods
- Less nutrient loss than refrigeration
- Just thaw and heat correctly

### Q2: How long do frozen foods last?

**Varies by food**:
- Meat: 1-2 months
- Grains: 1 month
- Vegetables: 1 month
- Soups: 2-3 months

### Q3: How to make freezer meals taste better?

**Tips**:
- Add fresh herbs after thawing
- Add water when microwaving
- Add fresh veggies when stir-frying
