# Diabetes Prevention: Building a Healthy Lifestyle Defense

Welcome to the Diabetes Prevention Section.

**Topic Hub**:
*   **[Pregnancy & Lactation Diabetes Cluster (Central Hub)](./../topic-cluster-pregnancy-lactation-en.md)**

Diabetes, especially Type 2 diabetes, can largely be prevented or delayed through active lifestyle interventions. Prevention is not just about avoiding a disease, but about investing in long-term health and quality of life. This section will provide scientific and practical prevention strategies from four core dimensions: **diet, exercise, sleep, and emotional health**.

Before starting, it is helpful to understand the diagnostic criteria for diabetes. You can refer to the authoritative materials we have compiled for you:
*   **[View: Global Major Diabetes Diagnostic Criteria](./../diabetes-diagnostic-criteria-en.md)**

### 2. [Regular Exercise](./exercise/)

Exercise is one of the most effective ways to improve insulin sensitivity and control weight. Regular physical activity can directly help the body burn excess sugar. We will share:
- **[Exercise as Medicine: How Scientific Training Effectively Prevents Diabetes](./exercise/how-daily-exercise-prevents-diabetes-en.md)**
- **[Pregnancy-Safe Exercise for Gestational Diabetes Prevention: Turn After-Meal Movement into a Daily Stabilizer](./exercise/pregnancy-safe-exercise-gestational-diabetes-prevention-en.md)**
- **Aerobic Exercise**: Like brisk walking, jogging, swimming, to improve cardiovascular function.
- **Resistance Training**: Like weight training, elastic band training, to increase muscle mass and improve basal metabolism.
- **Exercise Frequency and Duration**: According to international guidelines, at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity exercise per week is recommended.
- **Dangers of Sedentary Lifestyle**: How to find opportunities to move your body in daily work and life.
- **[Prediabetes Early Warning Signs: The Golden Window for Exercise Intervention](./exercise/prediabetes-early-warning-signs-en.md)**

- **Establishing Sleep Rhythm**: How to develop a regular sleep schedule.

Long-term stress and negative emotions can lead to elevated stress hormones, thereby affecting blood sugar levels. Emotion management is an often overlooked but crucial prevention aspect. We will discuss:
