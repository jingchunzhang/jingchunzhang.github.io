# Type 2 Diabetes Monitoring Protocols: A Practical Home Version

> **[中文版](./type2-diabetes-monitoring-protocols)** | **[Chinese Version](./type2-diabetes-monitoring-protocols)**

Good Type 2 management is not random checking—it is structured observation.

## Build your protocol
1. Define fixed test windows (fasting + post-meal).
2. Log food and activity with each data point.
3. Review weekly patterns before changing habits.
4. Prepare a concise summary for clinic appointments.

## What to bring to your next visit
- 2–4 weeks of trend logs.
- Repeated high/low pattern notes.
- Questions about medication timing and side effects.
