# Type 1 Diabetes: Turning Clinic Advice into a Daily Home Plan

> **[中文版](./type1-diabetes-clinic-to-home-plan)** | **[Chinese Version](./type1-diabetes-clinic-to-home-plan)**

Type 1 care can feel complex. A written daily protocol reduces decision fatigue and helps families respond faster.

## Daily structure
- Morning baseline check.
- Meal-by-meal monitoring and insulin discussion plan.
- Exercise-time glucose safety strategy.
- Night-time risk prevention checklist.

## Family safety checklist
1. Keep low-glucose rescue supplies in fixed locations.
2. Share emergency steps with family members.
3. Keep a short “what changed today” log for follow-up visits.
