# Low-Glycemic Cooking Practical Guide for Busy Families

> **[中文版](./low-glycemic-cooking-practical-guide)** | **[Chinese Version](./low-glycemic-cooking-practical-guide)**

Healthy cooking becomes sustainable when recipes are simple, repeatable, and family-friendly.

## Core plate logic
- Fiber first (vegetables, legumes).
- Protein anchor each meal.
- Smart carbohydrate portions.
- Cooking methods that reduce excess oil/sugar load.

## Batch-cooking ideas
1. Build two base proteins for 2–3 days.
2. Keep one high-fiber side ready.
3. Prepare low-sugar snack options in advance.
