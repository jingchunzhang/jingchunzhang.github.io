# Diabetes Prevention and Treatment Roadmap: A Step-by-Step Path

> **[中文版](./diabetes-prevention-treatment-roadmap)** | **[Chinese Version](./diabetes-prevention-treatment-roadmap)**

People get overwhelmed when they try to optimize everything at once. A staged roadmap keeps execution realistic.

## Phase 1: Detect risk early
- Family history and metabolic risk review.
- Baseline labs and blood pressure profile.

## Phase 2: Build sustainable habits
- Meal structure and movement minimums.
- Sleep and stress stabilization.

## Phase 3: Clinical coordination
- Scheduled follow-up windows.
- Medication/therapy discussion based on trend data.
