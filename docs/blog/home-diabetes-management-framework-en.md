# A Home Diabetes Management Framework You Can Actually Maintain

> **[中文版](./home-diabetes-management-framework)** | **[Chinese Version](./home-diabetes-management-framework)**

Consistency beats intensity in diabetes care. The goal is a routine you can keep for years.

## The 4-part framework
1. **Monitor**: track key glucose points on a stable schedule.
2. **Fuel**: build repeatable meals with fiber and balanced carbs.
3. **Move**: add short, regular movement windows.
4. **Review**: weekly reflection on patterns, not single readings.

## Weekly review prompts
- What time window gives your biggest glucose rise?
- Which meal pattern feels easiest to sustain?
- Where do stress and poor sleep affect control most?
