# Endocrinology Tests for Diabetes Follow-Up: What Matters Most

> **[中文版](./endocrinology-tests-for-diabetes-followup)** | **[Chinese Version](./endocrinology-tests-for-diabetes-followup)**

Follow-up testing is where long-term risk control happens. The key is trend interpretation, not one isolated value.

## Common follow-up priorities
- HbA1c trend over time.
- Kidney-related indicators and urine testing.
- Lipid profile and cardiovascular risk markers.
- Thyroid or endocrine panels when clinically indicated.

## How to read your trend better
1. Compare in fixed intervals.
2. Note medication, sleep, stress, and exercise changes.
3. Ask “what action does this trend trigger?” at each visit.
