# Late-Night Hunger Plan for Comfort Food in Diabetes Treatment

> **[中文版](./comfort-food-late-night-hunger-plan-diabetes-treatment)**

Nighttime is a high-risk moment for unplanned comfort eating. Use a preset flow:

- assess true hunger vs stress
- if hungry, choose protein + fiber first
- avoid sugary drinks and large refined-carb portions

Track next-morning fasting glucose to decide whether the snack strategy is working.
