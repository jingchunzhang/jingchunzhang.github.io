# Weekend Recovery Routine with Comfort Food in Diabetes Treatment

> **[中文版](./comfort-food-weekend-recovery-routine-diabetes-treatment)**

After a volatile week, you do not need aggressive restriction. Use a calm weekend reset:

1. Return to plate structure.
2. Replace snack spirals with warm high-fiber comfort meals.
3. Review one high-risk scenario for next week.
