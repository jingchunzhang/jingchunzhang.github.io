> **[中文版](./comfort-food-meal-prep-batch-cooking-diabetes-treatment)**

When schedules get busy, decisions get worse. Batch prep turns emergency eating into planned eating.

- 2-3 protein bases
- 3-4 vegetable bases
- 2 smart-carb bases

Assemble each meal using half-plate vegetables + quarter protein + quarter smart carbs.
