# Dessert Portion Strategy for Diabetes Treatment

> **[中文版](./comfort-food-dessert-portion-strategy-diabetes-treatment)**

The biggest dessert problem is not occasional sweetness. It is lack of boundaries.

- Keep dessert after meals, not on an empty stomach.
- Pre-define portion before eating.
- Pair with protein/fat to reduce rapid swings.
