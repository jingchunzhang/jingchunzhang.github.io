# Balancing Casseroles and Rice Bakes in Diabetes Treatment

> **[中文版](./comfort-food-casserole-rice-balance-diabetes-treatment)**

Casseroles can stay in treatment-stage eating if structure changes first.

1. Replace part of white rice with mixed grains or cauliflower rice.
2. Add visible protein in every serving.
3. Keep a large non-starchy vegetable side.
