# Snack and Craving Control in Diabetes Treatment

> **[中文版](./comfort-food-snack-craving-control-diabetes-treatment)**

Most treatment-stage derailments happen between meals, not during planned meals.

1. Assess: real hunger or stress trigger?
2. Substitute: water/tea + 10-minute walk.
3. If still hungry: protein + fiber snack.
