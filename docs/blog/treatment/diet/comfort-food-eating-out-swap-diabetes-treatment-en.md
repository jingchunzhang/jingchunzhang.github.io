# Eating-Out Swap Guide for Comfort Food in Diabetes Treatment

> **[中文版](./comfort-food-eating-out-swap-diabetes-treatment)**

Restaurant comfort food can still fit treatment-stage eating when swaps are planned.

1. Order protein/vegetables first.
2. Keep sauces on the side.
3. Default to unsweetened drinks.
4. If dessert, share a small planned portion.
