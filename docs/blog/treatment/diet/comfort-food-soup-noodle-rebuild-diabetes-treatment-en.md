# Rebuilding Comfort Soup Noodles During Diabetes Treatment

> **[中文版](./comfort-food-soup-noodle-rebuild-diabetes-treatment)**

Soup noodles are comforting, but often carb-heavy and protein-poor. You do not need to ban them; rebuild the bowl.

- Noodles: cut to half or two-thirds
- Add protein: egg/tofu/chicken/shrimp
- Vegetables: at least half the bowl volume
- Eat vegetables and protein first
