# Family Dinner Rotation for Diabetes Treatment Comfort Meals

> **[中文版](./comfort-food-family-dinner-rotation-diabetes-treatment)**

A major treatment-stage barrier is family friction, not knowledge. Weekly dinner rotation reduces conflict and fatigue.

- Mon: rebuilt soup noodles
- Tue: baked comfort tray with mixed grains
- Wed: stew + legume-forward carbs
- Thu: soup pot + measured starch
- Fri: planned comfort meal

Keep one shared meal base and adjust only carb type/portion by individual need.
