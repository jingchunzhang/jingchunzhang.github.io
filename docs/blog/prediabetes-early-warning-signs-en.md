# Prediabetes Early Warning Signs: What to Track This Month

> **[中文版](./prediabetes-early-warning-signs)** | **[Chinese Version](./prediabetes-early-warning-signs)**

Prediabetes is often quiet, but your numbers usually speak before symptoms do.

## Core warning signals
- Fasting glucose drifting upward across multiple checks.
- Post-meal spikes that stay elevated too long.
- Rising HbA1c over repeated tests.

## 30-day action focus
1. Keep a simple glucose-food-activity log.
2. Walk 10–20 minutes after major meals.
3. Replace high-sugar snacks with high-fiber alternatives.
4. Review your pattern weekly, not day-by-day emotionally.
