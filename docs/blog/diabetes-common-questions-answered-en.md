# Diabetes Common Questions Answered: A Practical FAQ

> **[中文版](./diabetes-common-questions-answered)** | **[Chinese Version](./diabetes-common-questions-answered)**

Newly diagnosed patients often ask the same core questions—and that is a good thing. Clear questions build safer routines.

## FAQ highlights
### Do I need to check glucose every day?
It depends on treatment plan and risk level. Consistency matters more than random over-checking.

### Can I still eat carbohydrates?
Yes. Focus on total pattern, quality, and portion—not complete elimination.

### Why do my numbers vary so much?
Meals, sleep, stress, activity, illness, and medication timing all interact.
