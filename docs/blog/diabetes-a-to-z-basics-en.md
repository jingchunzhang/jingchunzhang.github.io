# Diabetes A-to-Z Basics: A Practical Starting Point

> **[中文版](./diabetes-a-to-z-basics)** | **[Chinese Version](./diabetes-a-to-z-basics)**

If you are newly diagnosed or supporting someone with diabetes, an A-to-Z style reference can reduce panic and help you make better daily decisions.

## What to learn first
1. Your core numbers: fasting glucose, post-meal glucose, HbA1c.
2. Your daily pattern: food, activity, sleep, stress, medication timing.
3. Your warning signs: persistent highs, repeated lows, unusual symptoms.

## 7-day beginner checklist
- Log blood glucose consistently.
- Keep meals regular and portion-aware.
- Add light movement after meals.
- Record sleep and stress level.
- Prepare a hypoglycemia backup snack.
