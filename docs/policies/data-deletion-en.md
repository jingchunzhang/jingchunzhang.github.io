# Data Deletion and Account Closure

**Last Updated: March 16, 2026**

We respect your right to control your personal data. This page provides detailed information on how to request deletion of your personal data and account closure.

---

## 1. Your Data Deletion Rights

### 1.1 Applicable Regulations

Based on the following regulations, you have the right to request data deletion:

- **China Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL)**: Deletion right under Article 47
- **EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**: Article 17 "Right to be Forgotten"
- **US California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)**: Deletion Right

### 1.2 Types of Data That Can Be Deleted

- Account basic information (username, email, etc.)
- Subscription records and preferences
- Browsing history and cookies data
- Any information you actively submitted

---

## 2. Deletion Process

### 2.1 Submitting a Request

You can submit a data deletion request through:

1. **Email** (Recommended)
   - Email: privacy@tangyou.space
   - Subject: [Data Deletion Request]
   - See format below

2. **Written Request**
   - Send email to the address above
   - Include the necessary information below

### 2.2 Request Format

Please submit your request in the following format:

```
Subject: [Data Deletion Request]

Dear Tangyou Space Team:

I hereby request deletion of my personal data/account closure with the following details:

1. Registered Email: _______________
2. Username (if applicable): _______________
3. Registration Date (if known): _______________
4. Reason for Deletion (optional): _______________

I confirm the above information is true and authorize you to proceed with data deletion.

Applicant: _______________
Application Date: _______________
```

### 2.3 Processing Time

- **Acknowledgment**: We will acknowledge receipt within 3 business days
- **Processing**: We will complete data deletion within 30 days
- **Complex Cases**: For complex requests, processing may take up to 60 days (we will notify you in advance)

---

## 3. Effects After Deletion

### 3.1 Data That Cannot Be Recovered

After account closure, the following data will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered:

- Account information and profile
- Subscription records
- Purchase history (some transaction records may be retained per tax requirements)
- Browsing preferences and settings

### 3.2 Retained Data

The following data may be retained per legal requirements:

- Transaction records (for tax compliance, retained for 7 years)
- Security logs (for security audits, retained for 12 months)
- Other data required by law

---

## 4. Special Circumstances

### 4.1 Outstanding Orders

If your account has pending purchase orders:

- Account closure can only be completed after orders are fulfilled
- Or you can choose to delete other data only, retaining order information

### 4.2 Legal Disputes

If your data is involved in ongoing legal investigations or disputes:

- We may retain relevant data until the dispute is resolved
- We will inform you of the specific situation

### 4.3 Third-Party Shared Data

If your data has been shared with third parties:

- We will notify relevant third parties to delete your data
- Require recipients to comply with deletion requirements

---

## 5. Alternatives to Account Closure

If you do not wish to fully close your account, you can choose:

### 5.1 Unsubscribe Only

- Cancel all newsletter subscriptions
- Keep your account for future use

### 5.2 Data Export

- Export a backup of your data before deletion
- We can provide a copy of your personal data

### 5.3 Account Suspension

- Temporarily deactivate your account
- You can reactivate anytime later

---

## 6. Identity Verification

To protect your data security, we may need to verify your identity:

- Verification via registered email
- Request additional account information for confirmation
- Additional verification may be required for sensitive operations

---

## 7. Fees

Data deletion requests are **processed free of charge**. We will not charge any fees.

---

## 8. Cases Where Deletion May Be Denied

In rare circumstances, we may not be able to fulfill deletion requests:

- Involved in legal proceedings or investigations
- Related to public interest
- Data required to be retained by law

If we cannot fulfill the request, we will:

- Inform you of the reasons within 30 days
- Provide legal basis
- Suggest alternative solutions if applicable

---

## 9. Complaint Channels

If you are not satisfied with our data deletion handling:

- **Initial Complaint**: contact@tangyou.space
- **Regulatory Complaints**:
  - China: Cyberspace Administration of China
  - EU: Data Protection Authorities in respective member states
  - US: California Consumer Privacy Agency
